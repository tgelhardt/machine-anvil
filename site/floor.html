What the counters will mean

When this becomes real, every number maps to a mechanism.

Nothing on this page is live yet. The design intent: each counter is eventually derived from onchain or terminal-native activity — never from a central database the team can edit.

01

Terminals activated

Count of activated terminals, derived from activation events. Season 0 seats map to activations.

02

Shifts worked

Cumulative clock-in/clock-out sessions. Pace is fixed per terminal — nobody can mine faster than anyone else.

03

Receipts printed

Shift closeouts with a print size. Lottery mechanic: prints range from 44 up to 4,444 $BLANK — a big hit is the hook.

04

$BLANK mined

Total emitted, capped by the mine. Fixed supply of 3,469,000,000 — what's left shrinks as the floor works.

05

The index

Season progress and floor health indicator. 90/90 means a full index of seats and desks accounted for.

06

Desks

Reward routes: BNKR Rail is the primary modeled desk. ETH/USDG and Stock Desk stay gated until compliant rails exist.

The honesty line

Counters create motion, motion creates belief, belief creates demand. That is how the machine works — but the counters are mock data today, and the page says so. When they become real, they will be verifiable, not decorative.
