Gameplay

The game loop behind the Terminal Floor.

OK Brokers should feel like a trading-floor arcade running in the OK Computer command world: short actions, visible meters, native posts, rank progression, unlockable desks, and receipts that prove the terminal worked.

01

Shifts

A shift is an active session. It can map to gm/board/channel/data commands later, with a timer, output, receipt, and clock-out event.

02

$BLANK

$BLANK is the score and fuel. It is earned through shifts and can be designed with burns, tickets, boosts, and rank requirements.

03

Ranks

Observer → Trainee → Broker → Desk Captain → Pit Boss. Ranks give holders clear goals and explain why another shift matters.

04

Desks

Desks are unlockable counters. BNKR Rail opens first. ETH and USDG require higher status. Stock Desk remains eligibility-gated.

What makes it an arcade?

Not the word arcade. The loop. The holder should always know the next move: clock in, fill the meter, earn receipts, hit the next rank, unlock a desk, and compare progress. That creates repeat play without pretending rewards are guaranteed.
